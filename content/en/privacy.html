## data | 2. Data we collect

Account and identity data

  • Name, email address and (if you link it) phone number. If you link a phone number, a verification code is sent via Firebase Authentication so you can verify it and sign in by SMS.
  • The name and email address shared by Google or Apple when you sign in with them. If you use Apple’s "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address created by Apple.
  • The user ID created by the authentication service. Your password is stored by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it.

Farm and business data

  • Farm details you enter (farm name, business details, currency preference).
  • Animal records: ear tag number, species, breed, birth, lineage, group, health, vaccination, treatment, milk, breeding and pregnancy records.
  • Finance, feed, ration, inventory, calendar and paddock records.
  • Animal photos, files attached to health records and notes you choose to upload.

Team data

  • Names and contact details (email address or phone number) of people you invite to your team, their roles and permissions, and which team member or vet created which record.

Subscription data

  • Your plan, purchase and renewal status and transaction IDs. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; your card details are never shared with us or RevenueCat.

Device and technical data

  • Your device’s push notification token, so we can send notifications.
  • Device model, operating system and app version, language preference.
  • Security logs of requests to our servers (IP address, request time, error logs).

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your device’s GPS location.
  • There are no ads in the App; we do not collect your advertising ID, use your data for advertising or sell it.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools in the App.
  • Fingerprint and face recognition data stay on your device and are never sent to us.

## retention | 8. Retention
  • Account and farm data are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your account, the farms you own and their records are deleted from our systems. Deletion requests sent by email are completed within 30 days at the latest.
  • Security server logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted.
  • Data we are legally required to keep is retained for the statutory period and then deleted or anonymised.
## security | 9. Security

Data is transferred between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is limited by authentication and farm-level authorisation: each user can only access the farms they belong to, to the extent their role allows. No system is completely risk-free, but we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.
