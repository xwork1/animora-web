## in-app | 1. Delete in the app (recommended)
  1. Open the Animora app and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings from the bottom menu.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the end of the list.
  4. Confirm in the dialog. Your account and data are deleted and you are signed out.

This cannot be undone. Before deleting, you can export records you want to keep as PDF or Excel from the reports section.

## email | 2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app (e.g. you lost your device or uninstalled the app), send a deletion request by email:

  • To: {{emailAddr}}
  • Subject: Animora account deletion request
  • Include: the email address registered in the app and your farm name (if any).

Please send the request from your registered email address if possible so we can confirm the account is yours. We may ask additional questions to verify your identity. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest and we confirm the result by email.

## partial | 3. Delete specific data without deleting your account

To keep your account but remove some data, you can delete individual animal, health, milk or finance records in the app. To delete a specific group of data (e.g. all photos or all records of a farm), email us with the subject "Animora data deletion request".

## what | 4. What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted

  • Account and profile details (name, email, phone)
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication record)
  • Farms you own and all their records (animals, health, milk, breeding, finance, feed, inventory, calendar, paddocks)
  • Photos you uploaded
  • Your device’s notification registration

May be kept

  • Records we must keep by law, for the statutory period
  • Security server access logs, for a limited period
  • Purchase records, kept by Apple and Google under their own policies

Data is deleted or anonymised when its retention period ends.

## subscription | 5. About your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription. To avoid further charges, cancel it in your store settings before deleting your account: App Store subscriptions · Google Play subscriptions.

## team | 6. Team members

If you are a team member or veterinarian on another user’s farm, deleting your account ends your access to those farms. As those farms’ records belong to the farm owner, records you created may remain as the farm owner’s data.
