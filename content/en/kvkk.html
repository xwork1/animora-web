## collection | 5. Method of collection

Your personal data is collected electronically and partly by automated means through the Animora mobile app, sign-in providers (Google, Apple), the verification code sent via WhatsApp for phone sign-in, app stores, the subscription infrastructure and email.

## transfers | 6. Transfers of personal data

Limited to the purposes above, your personal data may be transferred:

  • Within Türkiye: to competent public authorities upon request,
  • Abroad: to the service providers Google LLC (Firebase) for authentication and notifications, Apple Inc. for Sign in with Apple, WhatsApp LLC / Meta Platforms, Inc. for delivering the phone sign-in verification code as a WhatsApp message from Appouse’s WhatsApp business line (through our self-hosted OpenWA gateway) and RevenueCat, Inc. for subscription management.

Transfers abroad are carried out in line with the procedures and safeguards set out in KVKK article 9 (including appropriate safeguards and, where required, standard contracts). As part of team membership, your records can only be viewed by people you have authorised on the same farm.

## retention | 7. Retention period

Your personal data is kept while your account is active and for as long as the purpose of processing requires. When the account is deleted or the purpose no longer applies, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised, subject to statutory retention periods.

## rights | 8. Your rights under KVKK article 11

By applying to the Company, you have the right to:

  1. learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. request information if it has been processed,
  3. learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  4. know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  5. request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  6. request deletion or destruction under KVKK article 7,
  7. request that operations under (e) and (f) are notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  8. object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  9. claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.
