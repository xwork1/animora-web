## account | Sign-in and account

I forgot my password

Use the "Forgot?" link on the email sign-in screen to receive a reset link. If you signed up with Google, Apple or your phone number, sign in the same way.

I want to delete my account

In the app go to Settings → Delete Account. Details and email deletion: Account and Data Deletion.

I did not receive the phone sign-in code

The verification code is sent as a WhatsApp message from Appouse’s WhatsApp business line, not by SMS. Make sure WhatsApp is installed and active on the number you entered and that the number includes the correct country code. The code is valid for 5 minutes; request a new one if it has expired.

## subscriptions | Subscriptions and payments

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your store account: App Store subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

I changed devices and my subscription is missing

Sign in with the same Animora account on the new device and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. You must be signed in with the Apple or Google account used for the purchase.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from the store: Apple — Report a Problem · Google Play refunds.

## notifications | Notifications

If reminders do not arrive, check that notifications are enabled for Animora in your device settings and that battery saver is not restricting the app.
