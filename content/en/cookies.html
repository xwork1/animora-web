## no-cookies | 1. We do not use cookies

This website does not use cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That is why we do not show a cookie banner.

The site serves its fonts and all files from its own server and does not load content from external services such as Google Fonts.

## logs | 2. Server logs

When you visit the site, the web server keeps standard access logs for security and operation: IP address, date and time, requested page and browser details. These logs are not used to identify you or build profiles and are deleted after a limited period.

## links | 3. External links

The site may link to third-party sites such as the App Store, Google Play or Apple. Their own cookie and privacy policies apply when you visit them.
